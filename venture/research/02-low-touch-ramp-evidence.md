## Ramp conclusions
- Most products never reach $1k MRR. Of those that do, the median time is about 8 months.
- $10k MRR is a top ~5% outcome within 2 years. Base rates are falling as AI-assisted launches flood markets.
- Part-time founders grow slower.

## Agent's realistic target
- Months 0–3: first payers, $0–300/mo.
- Month 12: $500–2k/mo if it works. Most likely outcome for any single product is under $1k/mo.
- Years 2–3: $5–10k/mo is top-quartile.
- **$40k/mo in 1–3 months from <$1k: no credible evidence.**

## Model ranking from this source
B2B vertical micro-SaaS and paid data/API products rank above SEO directories and Chrome extensions, because the latter carry platform and AI-Overview risk. Templates/digital downloads: no credible revenue data found; not recommended.
