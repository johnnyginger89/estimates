## Conflict analysis (assumptions, not evidence)
- A self-serve product at $50/mo needs roughly 900+ paying customers to net $40k/mo. At $200/mo it needs ~240+.
- At an assumed 5%/mo churn, that also means replacing ~12–45 customers every month just to stay flat.
- With <$1k there is no meaningful paid acquisition, so growth would have to be organic, which is slow.
- 1–2 h/week rules out owner-led sales, which rules out high-price/low-volume models.
- Conclusion: no credible path to $40k/mo in 1–3 months under these constraints.
