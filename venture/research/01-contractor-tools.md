## Candidate sketches
- **A. Quote follow-up add-on** ($15–29/mo) for Jobber Core / Joist / spreadsheet users. The gap is confirmed on Jobber's own pricing page. Risks: Jobber can close the gap; integration not yet verified; no customer-voice evidence found.
- **B. Painter instant-quote widget** ($19–39/mo). There is price headroom, but existing near-identical sellers show no traction.
- **C. Production-rate estimator workbook** ($29–79 one-time). Very low maintenance, but low ceiling and high AI/free competition.
- **D. Job-costing tracker.** Weak: Housecall Pro and free templates already cover it.
- **Ruled out:** review-request tools (crowded).

## Agent's assessment
A has the strongest evidence. Biggest unknowns: will users pay a third party instead of upgrading or using DIY, and can a product hook into Jobber quote status without manual forwarding? Acquisition, not the build, is the binding constraint.
