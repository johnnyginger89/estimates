## Conclusions
- No channel reliably delivers paying customers to a new product without effort. "List it and it sells" is not supported by 2026 evidence.
- SEO and free-tool traffic is weakened by AI Overviews. Expect 6–12+ months for a new domain (inference).
- Payments: use a merchant of record (Paddle 5% + 50¢, or Stripe Managed Payments), so GST/PST/VAT and disputes are handled.
- Tax: Johnny is probably already GST-registered through Grandview if he's a sole proprietor over $30k. He may not qualify as a BC PST small seller. **Confirm both with an accountant before selling.**
